Three shortcuts

Hold Ctrl Alt and Tellsy does the rest.

Tellsy sits next to the clock and works in whatever app you're in: Chrome, Gmail, WhatsApp, Word, a PDF. Every shortcut can be changed.

Read

Ctrl Alt R for a selection, P for the whole page

Select some text and Tellsy reads it to you in a natural voice, lighting up each line on the page as it goes, so you never lose your place.

A long email, an article, a contract clause.Speed up with ., slow down with ,

Ask

Hold Ctrl Alt A, ask, let go

Ask about what's on screen in your own words. Tellsy answers out loud.

“Summarize this in two sentences.”“Translate this to Hindi.” “Is this email rude?”

Reply

Hold Ctrl Alt D, speak, let go

Click into any text box and say what you want to write. Tellsy types it, cleaned up, as you finish speaking.

“Sounds good um let's do Thursday no wait Friday at five”becomes “Sounds good, let's do Friday at five.”

Privacy

Nothing leaves your computer until you hold a key.

Reading happens on your PC. Ask and Reply send your voice to Google Gemini only while you hold their shortcut, using your own free key.

  • Offline voicesReading aloud uses Piper neural voices that run on your own computer. No internet needed.
  • The mic is off until you hold Ask or ReplyLet go and it stops. Tellsy never listens in the background.
  • Your key stays with youYour Gemini key is saved in a file on your computer and goes only to Google.
  • Your clipboard comes backAfter Tellsy types a reply, whatever you had copied is put back.
